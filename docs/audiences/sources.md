## Claim-to-source notes

These are the shortest paths from an external claim to its owner documentation. The linked Docket sections state the proposed application and its evidentiary limit.

### Original public acts

DEC ENB, SEQR, DPS DMM, NYISO and IDA records can locate a proposal, decision or revision. An ENB repost can repeat the lead agency’s act; a filing is not an order; a queue request is not built capacity.

[DEC ENB and its coverage notes](https://dec.ny.gov/news/environmental-notice-bulletin/help) · [SEQR process](https://dec.ny.gov/regulatory/permits-licenses/seqr) · [DPS DMM search](https://documents.dps.ny.gov/search/Home/DocumentSearch) · [NYISO interconnections](https://www.nyiso.com/interconnections) · [IDA directory and reports](https://abo.ny.gov/industrial-development-agency-directory-and-reports) · [EIA-860M generator data](https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/) · [Where Docket uses it](./public-signal-atlas.md#official-decisions)

### Comparative Agendas Project (CAP)

CAP codes government policy activity. A proposed policy-attention overlay requires an actual licensed corpus, a versioned codebook and row-level mapping; it cannot establish a site permit or causal effect. CAP-generated variables carry noncommercial reuse conditions.

[CAP overview](https://www.comparativeagendas.net/pages/About) · [Master codebook](https://www.comparativeagendas.net/pages/master-codebook) · [Datasets](https://www.comparativeagendas.net/datasets_codebooks) · [Copyright and legal terms](https://www.comparativeagendas.net/pages/Copyright-and-Legal) · [Where Docket uses it](./issue-bridges.md#3-cap-policy-attention-connect-agendas-to-acts-without-confusing-them)

### Computational Antitrust

Stanford CodeX is a research reference for computational methods. Docket’s public-procurement dependency test is its own proposed application. Shared suppliers or similar bids alone establish no violation, and neither Stanford nor DOJ has endorsed Docket.

[Stanford CodeX project](https://law.stanford.edu/codex-the-stanford-center-for-legal-informatics/projects/computational-antitrust/) · [DOJ Procurement Collusion Strike Force](https://www.justice.gov/atr/procurement-collusion-strike-force) · [Where Docket uses it](./issue-bridges.md#2-computational-antitrust-inspect-dependencies-without-manufacturing-collusion)

### Digital public infrastructure and procurement

UNDP describes interoperable, governed foundational digital systems; OCDS describes contracting stages. The receiving-side service and supplier-exit test is a Docket proposal, not proof that Docket is DPI or that an actual city contract permits transfer.

[UNDP DPI overview](https://www.undp.org/digital/digital-public-infrastructure) · [OCDS process](https://standard.open-contracting.org/latest/en/primer/how/) · [GovStack specifications](https://specs.govstack.global/) · [Where Docket uses it](./issue-bridges.md#4-dpi-and-municipal-procurement-make-public-computing-usable-after-a-supplier-changes)

### Outside-the-fence network observations

RIS/RouteViews BGP, RIPE Atlas measurements, DNS, CT, RDAP and voluntary peering entries may suggest what to check next. They do not count as independent official acts, locate a data center or reveal its private traffic.

[RIPE RIS](https://ris.ripe.net/docs/mrt/) · [RouteViews API](https://api.routeviews.org/docs/) · [RIPE Atlas](https://atlas.ripe.net/docs/apis/rest-api-manual/introduction) · [ARIN RDAP](https://www.arin.net/resources/registry/whois/rdap/) · [PeeringDB search](https://docs.peeringdb.com/howto/search/) · [Certificate Transparency](https://googlechrome.github.io/CertificateTransparency/) · [Where Docket uses it](./public-signal-atlas.md#internet-observations)

### Network data reuse rights

Read access is not a commercial redistribution license. Check the rights for the exact source and use before designing a product adapter.

[RIPE RIS commercial use](https://www.ripe.net/analyse/internet-measurements/routing-information-service-ris/commercial-use/) · [RIPEstat terms](https://www.ripe.net/about-us/legal/ripestat-service-terms-and-conditions/) · [RIPE Atlas terms](https://www.ripe.net/about-us/legal/ripe-atlas-service-terms-and-conditions/) · [ARIN Whois terms](https://www.arin.net/resources/registry/whois/tou/) · [PeeringDB acceptable use](https://www.peeringdb.com/aup) · [Where Docket uses it](./public-signal-atlas.md#read-the-boundary-correctly)

### Repair, assurance and permissioned telemetry

OCP documents design and firmware-security programs; an FM data sheet gives loss-prevention guidance; the FTC repair report is background. None grants repair rights, access to an operator’s logs, insurance coverage or a certified Docket outcome.

[OCP products and recognition](https://www.opencompute.org/products) · [FTC Nixing the Fix report](https://www.ftc.gov/reports/nixing-fix-ftc-report-congress-repair-restrictions) · [FM loss-prevention sheets](https://www.fm.com/resources/fm-data-sheets) · [OpenTelemetry documentation](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/) · [Where Docket uses it](./issue-bridges.md#5-insurance-and-assurance-test-a-defined-loss-and-a-real-recovery-path)

## Verification method and gaps

- The September 30 link sweep attempted 129 distinct external URLs used in the eight reader-facing pages before this edit. It received 112 HTTP 2xx responses. Three clear 404 destinations were replaced (Accela permitting, an ICANN DNS explainer and an old NY IDA directory). 403, 502 and timeout responses were **not** treated as evidence that the owner page is false or gone; some sites block automated clients. Important owner descriptions and terms were checked separately against primary pages.
- A working URL is not proof of every sentence beside it. In particular, public portal pages cannot establish a named site, a CAP row, legal liability, a license grant, market price, endorsement or an operational Docket capability. Provider descriptions are first-party feature claims, not independent performance tests.
- The bibliography is a dated editorial index. For a published case or procurement decision, pin the exact underlying document, version, jurisdiction, right to reuse, adverse evidence and a reviewer’s decision. [Project status](../../STATUS.md) remains the capability authority.
