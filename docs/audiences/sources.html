Claim-to-source notes

Original public acts

DEC ENB, SEQR, DPS DMM, NYISO and IDA records can locate a proposal, decision or revision. An ENB repost can repeat the lead agency’s act; a filing is not an order; a queue request is not built capacity.

DEC ENB and its coverage notes ↗ · SEQR process ↗ · DPS DMM search ↗ · NYISO interconnections ↗ · IDA directory and reports ↗ · EIA-860M generator data ↗ · Docket use ↗

Comparative Agendas Project (CAP)

CAP codes government policy activity. A proposed policy-attention overlay requires an actual licensed corpus, a versioned codebook and row-level mapping; it cannot establish a site permit or causal effect. CAP-generated variables carry noncommercial reuse conditions.

CAP overview ↗ · Master codebook ↗ · Datasets ↗ · Copyright and legal terms ↗ · Docket use ↗

Computational Antitrust

Stanford CodeX is a research reference for computational methods. Docket’s public-procurement dependency test is its own proposed application. Shared suppliers or similar bids alone establish no violation, and neither Stanford nor DOJ has endorsed Docket.

Stanford CodeX project ↗ · DOJ Procurement Collusion Strike Force ↗ · Docket use ↗

Digital public infrastructure and procurement

UNDP describes interoperable, governed foundational digital systems; OCDS describes contracting stages. The receiving-side service and supplier-exit test is a Docket proposal, not proof that Docket is DPI or that an actual city contract permits transfer.

UNDP DPI overview ↗ · OCDS process ↗ · GovStack specifications ↗ · Docket use ↗

Outside-the-fence network observations

RIS/RouteViews BGP, RIPE Atlas measurements, DNS, CT, RDAP and voluntary peering entries may suggest what to check next. They do not count as independent official acts, locate a data center or reveal its private traffic.

RIPE RIS ↗ · RouteViews API ↗ · RIPE Atlas ↗ · ARIN RDAP ↗ · PeeringDB search ↗ · Certificate Transparency ↗ · Docket use ↗

Network data reuse rights

Read access is not a commercial redistribution license. Check the rights for the exact source and use before designing a product adapter.

RIPE RIS commercial use ↗ · RIPEstat terms ↗ · RIPE Atlas terms ↗ · ARIN Whois terms ↗ · PeeringDB acceptable use ↗ · Docket use ↗

Repair, assurance and permissioned telemetry

OCP documents design and firmware-security programs; an FM data sheet gives loss-prevention guidance; the FTC repair report is background. None grants repair rights, access to an operator’s logs, insurance coverage or a certified Docket outcome.
