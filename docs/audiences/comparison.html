Competitive output map · 29 September 2026

One public case.
Many useful outputs.

Data Center Docket aims to turn scattered official acts and outside-the-fence signals into a source-linked case, then produce the brief each audience needs. This is a narrower job than operating a data center—and one that existing trackers, policy tools and document workbenches already address in part.

The commercial opening is a repeatable, reviewable public decision workflow. Its speed and price advantage remain hypotheses to test.

03 / Price and time

Lower cash cost is possible. The discount is not yet known.

Current public examples are already mixed: Civitar has a free cited briefing tier; NetBox and openDCIM have open-source code; enterprise platforms often require a quote; Sunbird and Hyperview publish unit-based pricing. Those products do different work. No honest percentage can compare Docket's public memo with live DCIM monitoring.

Compare one identical reviewed output

Enter all six values to compare monthly cost. This worksheet makes no vendor savings claim.

Four price cases

  1. Official portals + manual memo: often no license fee, but retrieval, matching, citation and correction consume staff time.
  2. Free trackers and workbenches: can already supply maps, briefings or document search; compare against them first.
  3. Paid tracker or policy platform: may win immediately on coverage, alerts, market data or a mature memo workflow.
  4. Docket pilot: AGPL code and eligible public inputs can lower license cash, while operating, rights, OCR/LLM, maintenance and human review still cost.

Total monthly comparison = license / access + (reviewed cases × analyst minutes ÷ 60 × hourly cost) + hosting / model / maintenance + rights and QA.

The worksheet omits one-time setup and error/correction cost; include those in a purchasing decision. A lower number does not mean equal coverage or quality.

Speed test: start at the same public-record posting, then time to a correct reviewed memo. Record missing acts, false joins, exact citations, source independence and corrections. A generated draft in seconds is not a faster accountable output if a human spends longer repairing it.
