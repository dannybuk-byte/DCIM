# Data Center Docket research demonstrator

Standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript research prototype, compiled 30 September 2026. Open `index.html` in a browser. It has no external runtime dependencies; state lasts only for the session. Original-source links open external public websites when selected.

The neighborhood entry leads into seven buildout stages, evidence/catalogue/connections/investigation views, linked chronology/graph/coverage, and six evidence challenges. Four distinct case scopes are retained: 60 Hudson's first hall, the NYCyberCenter proposal, Apple Viborg/Foulum and Apple Athenry. The catalogue contains 61 candidate source families, including conditional/restricted references, rather than live feeds. Jobs has four sections, explicit unknown counts, source-reported work types, 41 searchable union/training routes, and dated institutional union-presence accounts.

## Sources and limitations

Each case record links to the public original or identifies a secondary publisher, with a locator and inference limit. Operator/contractor releases report their own work; union publications report their own investigations or agreements. Government hosting does not convert developer testimony into independent agency findings. Repeated records from one origin do not establish independent corroboration. The historical court account uses a third-party legal publisher; an official court-hosted copy has not been obtained.

The HTML contains short curated summaries, not source-document dumps. Exact passages and source versions were not independently reverified for this public copy. It contains no original capture receipts or completed-review certification. Source compilation dates do not establish first public availability. Strict historical replay excludes records whose availability is unknown. Current workforce context is separately labeled; program intakes, caps and role pages are not site jobs, guaranteed admission or complete union coverage. Historical records do not establish current site bargaining status.

The original pages and their reuse/access terms remain authoritative. The public catalogue/bibliography links provide research context, not admission decisions. Policy links point to the official [A11560 bill](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/A11560) and [Executive Order 62](https://www.governor.ny.gov/executive-order/no-62-establishing-temporary-moratorium-data-centers-new-york-while-state-develops). Their pages were accessible on 30 September 2026; this prototype makes no legal applicability determination or automatic status refresh. Other source endpoints were not comprehensively rechecked in this publication pass.
